RULE 1 - THE 50/30/20 BUDGET RULE:
A healthy monthly budget divides after-tax income into three buckets: 50% for Needs (rent, groceries, utility bills, basic transport), 30% for Wants (dining out, food delivery, shopping, movies), and 20% for Savings and Investments. If Wants exceed 30% of total income, the user is overspending.

RULE 2 - FOOD DELIVERY & DINING CAP:
Spending on restaurants and food delivery apps (like Swiggy or Zomato) should ideally stay below 10% of monthly income. Frequent small orders under ₹600 add up quickly and silently drain monthly savings.

RULE 3 - SUBSCRIPTION AUDIT:
Recurring auto-pay subscriptions (like Netflix, Spotify, Prime, or unused apps) should be reviewed monthly. If entertainment and subscriptions exceed 5% of income, cancel at least one unused service.

RULE 4 - IMPULSE SHOPPING CONTROL:
Non-essential online shopping (fashion, gadgets, impulse marketplace buys) should not exceed 10% to 12% of monthly income. Applying a 48-hour waiting rule before purchasing items over ₹1,500 cuts impulse spending by half.

RULE 5 - EMERGENCY FUND PRIORITY:
Every earner should save at least 20% of monthly income until they build an emergency fund covering 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses (rent, bills, groceries).
